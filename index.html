ℹ️ Scientific Analysis & FAQ

Will Tashkent drown?

No, central Tashkent is safe. The city center and northern districts sit on terraces 20–50 m above the Chirchik riverbed. Gauge depth at center is 0.00 m. Inundation is confined to the Chirchik river floodplain and southern lowlands (Bektemir, Qo'yliq, Sergeli).

How long until water reaches Tashkent?

3.5 to 4 hours. The river distance exceeds 60 km and wave front moves at 12–18 km/h. Rumors of «wiped out in 10–20 minutes» are scientifically impossible.

Downstream arrival timeline:

• Gazalkent: ~1 h (17–23 m)
• Chirchik: ~2 h (16–21 m)
• Tashkent (Qo'yliq): ~3.5–4 h
• Sergeli: ~4.5–5 h
• Chinaz: ~18 h
• Shardara (Kazakhstan): ~24 h

Scientific methodology:

SERGHEI (2D SWE, Roe, 60 m grid) and LISFLOOD-FP 8.1 (90 m). Froehlich (2008) model: peak discharge 182,013 m³/s, volume 2.0 km³.
